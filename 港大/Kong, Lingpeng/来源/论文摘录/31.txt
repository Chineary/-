Figure 1: A simplified demonstration of our benchmark. Actions will either occupy the agent or leave it idle. The
four steps of brewing must be executed sequentially as time constraints between actions. The goal for the agent is to
plan multitasking to complete the recipes in the shortest time possible without violating any constraints. The first
plan illustrates a scenario where the agent attempts always to keep the agent occupied for higher efficiency, resulting
in violations of time constraints. The second plan maintains the balance between the efficiency and feasibility of the
plan by leaving the agent idle on purpose to maintain the time constraints for all actions.
